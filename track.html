Who moved first

Model versus market, same-direction moves of half a point or more, lags of one to three days

For each candidate, a model move counts as first when the market makes a same-direction move of at least 0.5 points one to three days later. It counts as after when the market made that move one to three days earlier. Same-day matches are counted separately, since the model reruns on that morning's prices and usually moves with them. Day pairs that span a gap in history.csv, and days that carry a structural note (a model rebuild), are left out. A move with matching market moves both before and after is split half and half. Market never followed means the market made no matching move within three days either way. Losing days stay in the table.

Every call is a dated claim with a metric, a threshold and a deadline. It resolves true the first day the condition holds and false at its deadline if it never does.
