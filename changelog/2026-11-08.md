The 2028 Machine, week of November 8, 2026

The biggest market moves between November 1, 2026 and November 8, 2026 were Jon Ossoff, up 10.4 points to 25.5, Gavin Newsom, down 5.3 points to 11.3, Raphael Warnock, up 1.6 points to 3.7 and Abdul El-Sayed, up 0.8 points to 1.8. Everyone else moved less than that, and most of the field did not move at all.

The model’s disagreement with the market changed most for Jon Ossoff, where the gap between model share and market price went from -5.0 to -16.7, Gavin Newsom, where the gap between model share and market price went from 6.8 to 13.2 and Abdul El-Sayed, where the gap between model share and market price went from 4.7 to 2.9. A widening gap means the machine and the traders are reading the same news differently.

Threshold watch: Jon Ossoff fell below 10 in model share (10.1 to 8.8), Josh Shapiro fell below 5 in market price (5.0 to 4.5), Pete Buttigieg crossed above 5 in market price (4.9 to 5.3) and Abdul El-Sayed fell below 5 in model share (5.7 to 4.7). Five and ten are the lines where a candidate stops being a footnote and starts being a lane.

On the question of who actually runs, the Kalshi run market moved Chris Murphy down 31.0 points to 37 percent, Abdul El-Sayed down 21.0 points to 19 percent, Josh Shapiro up 11.0 points to 87 percent and Wes Moore up 11.0 points to 80 percent.

The machine reruns 20,000 primaries every morning on that day’s Kalshi and Polymarket prices and logs the result, so this is a record of what it believed and when, not a forecast.
