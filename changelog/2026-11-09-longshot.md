The 2028 Machine, long-shot correction of 2026-11-09

Before the market blend anchors the model, each book now gets a long-shot correction. A price under 5 is multiplied by (price / 5) to the power 0.5. Prices at 5 and above are untouched. The book, counting other as 100 minus the named prices, is then rescaled to 100, which lifts every price at 5 and above by the same factor. The corrected books are averaged by the same rule as the raw ones. The curve lives in index.html (longshot, with LONGSHOT.floor 5 and LONGSHOT.power 0.5) and runs the same in the nightly job and the browser.

Why: prediction markets overprice long shots and underprice favorites. A pure power curve could not meet both targets on the 2026-11-09 books (any power that takes a 2 down to 1.5 pushes a 26 past 32), and neither could a linear correction in log odds. The cutoff at 5 with a square root does: a 2 becomes 1.4 on both books and a 26 becomes 28.4 to 28.8.
