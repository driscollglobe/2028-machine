The 2028 Machine, week of October 25, 2026

The biggest market moves between October 18, 2026 and October 25, 2026 were Jon Ossoff, up 2.0 points to 15.7, Gavin Newsom, up 1.3 points to 16.0, AOC, up 1.2 points to 15.6 and Kamala Harris, down 0.7 points to 6.8. Everyone else moved less than that, and most of the field did not move at all.

The model’s disagreement with the market changed most for Gavin Newsom, where the gap between model share and market price went from 9.3 to 6.2, Jon Ossoff, where the gap between model share and market price went from -4.0 to -5.9 and Kamala Harris, where the gap between model share and market price went from 3.0 to 4.5. A widening gap means the machine and the traders are reading the same news differently.

Threshold watch: Pete Buttigieg fell below 5 in market price (5.0 to 4.9). Five and ten are the lines where a candidate stops being a footnote and starts being a lane.

On the question of who actually runs, the Kalshi run market moved Raphael Warnock up 10.0 points to 58 percent, Jon Ossoff up 8.0 points to 60 percent, Jon Stewart down 8.0 points to 7 percent and Wes Moore down 5.0 points to 73 percent.

The machine reruns 20,000 primaries every morning on that day’s Kalshi and Polymarket prices and logs the result, so this is a record of what it believed and when, not a forecast.
