The 2028 Machine, name ID of 2026-11-09

Name ID is now a model input. Each candidate's operator strength, before the market blend, is multiplied by the square root of the share of voters who have heard of them and by 1 plus half their net favorable among Democrats (clamped to 50 points either way). Name ID grows each simulated month by the growth rate times the attention score over 10 times the news index (last week's Google News count over the field median, capped at 3) times the share who haven't heard of them yet. The clock sets how many months pass before each contest.

Starting name ID is heard-of from the newest Economist/YouGov wave that asked about each candidate (scripts/fetch_nameid.py, nameid.csv, nightly after the market fetch). YouGov has no reading for 10 of 25 candidates; they start at 15 percent plus 5 points per attention point, capped at 60, and their field cards say estimated. Attention scores are operator priors in attention_scores.csv, reviewed Nov. 2026.

Five new settings in knobs.json: Name ID growth rate 0.047, fitted to Pete Buttigieg going from 15 percent in January 2019 to 80 percent at the Iowa caucus (node scripts/fit_nameid.js); Favorability weight 0.5; Estimated name ID floor 0.15; Estimated name ID per attention point 0.05; Estimated name ID ceiling 0.6.

On the 2026-11-09 books at default settings, 50,000 runs, the top five went from AOC 25.2 (likely 14 to 37), Ossoff 24.3 (13 to 38), Newsom 18.7 (9 to 32), Khanna 7.3 (5 to 10), Harris 5.2 (2 to 9) to AOC 26.8 (15 to 38), Ossoff 25.3 (14 to 39), Newsom 20.3 (9 to 34), Khanna 6.3 (4 to 8), Harris 5.4 (2 to 10). Still a two-way race. The convention rate went from 10.9 to 10.7.
