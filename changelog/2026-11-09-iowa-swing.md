The 2028 Machine, Iowa swing of 2026-11-09

Iowa crashing the window used to move Buttigieg's chance of finishing first by 71 points. We traced why. It was not momentum. With momentum switched off, the swing was still 72. Two things carried it. First, a calendar bonus: under the Iowa calendar Buttigieg got 1.4 times his strength in every contest through June, and under the DNC order he got 0.7 times. Second, his Iowa and New Hampshire fits of 1.5. Under the DNC order he was out after South Carolina and Nevada in 97 percent of runs. Under the Iowa calendar he survived the early window in 89 percent and finished first in 72.

Two rules changed. The calendar bonus now applies only to the early window, not to Super Tuesday or the long spring. And no single contest can multiply a candidate's strength by more than 1.2 or cut it below 1 divided by 1.2, counting the state fit and the calendar bonus together. Kevin chose 1.2 on 2026-11-09 because it is the loosest cap that holds both calendar levers under 25 points for every candidate.

The chain now, on the 2026-11-09 books (scripts/iowa_trace.js): Buttigieg wins Iowa in 20 percent of his runs, carries a momentum multiplier of 2.05 out of the early window when he survives it, wins New Hampshire in 22 percent, takes 34 percent of the South Carolina vote, and holds 79 delegates after the early window against 33 under the DNC order. He finishes first in 35 percent of runs against 12 percent. The swing is 22 points. On the nightly heatmap Iowa moves Buttigieg 11 points and its biggest mover is Ossoff, down 13.

The cap also trims South Carolina for Harris, whose fit there was 1.6 before the calendar bonus. On the 2026-11-09 books at default settings, 20,000 runs, the top five went from Ossoff 29.3, AOC 20.4, Newsom 19.0, Harris 11.4, Khanna 5.7 to AOC 25.0, Ossoff 22.7, Newsom 18.3, Khanna 9.6, Harris 5.0.
