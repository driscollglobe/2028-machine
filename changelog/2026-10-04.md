The 2028 Machine, week of October 4, 2026

The biggest market moves between September 27, 2026 and October 4, 2026 were AOC, down 1.2 points to 15.6, Abdul El-Sayed, down 1.0 points to 1.0, Gavin Newsom, up 0.5 points to 15.6 and Rahm Emanuel, down 0.4 points to 2.2. Everyone else moved less than that, and most of the field did not move at all.

The model’s disagreement with the market changed most for Abdul El-Sayed, where the gap between model share and market price went from 2.7 to 4.5, AOC, where the gap between model share and market price went from 22.9 to 22.1 and Ro Khanna, where the gap between model share and market price went from 3.3 to 3.8. A widening gap means the machine and the traders are reading the same news differently.

Threshold watch: Jon Ossoff fell below 10 in model share (10.1 to 9.8), Ro Khanna crossed above 5 in model share (4.9 to 5.5) and Abdul El-Sayed crossed above 5 in model share (4.7 to 5.5). Five and ten are the lines where a candidate stops being a footnote and starts being a lane.

On the question of who actually runs, the Kalshi run market moved Tim Walz down 15.0 points to 4 percent, Wes Moore down 8.0 points to 71 percent, Kamala Harris up 5.0 points to 58 percent and Gretchen Whitmer down 5.0 points to 27 percent.

The machine reruns 20,000 primaries every morning on that day’s Kalshi and Polymarket prices and logs the result, so this is a record of what it believed and when, not a forecast.
