The 2028 Machine, week of 2026-11-22

The biggest market moves between 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-22 were Jon Ossoff, down 3.6 points to 22.6, AOC, down 0.7 points to 14.1, Ro Khanna, down 0.7 points to 1.5 and Raphael Warnock, up 0.7 points to 3.9. Everyone else moved less than that, and most of the field did not move at all.

The model’s disagreement with the market changed most for AOC, where the gap between model share and market price went from 15.7 to 17.9, Gavin Newsom, where the gap between model share and market price went from 10.9 to 10.2 and Ro Khanna, where the gap between model share and market price went from 1.7 to 2.4. A widening gap means the machine and the traders are reading the same news differently.

Threshold watch: Gavin Newsom fell below 10 in market price (10.2 to 9.5) and Pete Buttigieg crossed above 5 in model share (4.5 to 5.1). Five and ten are the lines where a candidate stops being a footnote and starts being a lane.

On the question of who actually runs, the Kalshi run market moved Rahm Emanuel down 16.0 points to 70 percent, Mark Kelly down 13.0 points to 65 percent, Jon Ossoff down 10.0 points to 54 percent and Ruben Gallego down 8.0 points to 61 percent.

The machine reruns 50,000 primaries every morning on that day’s Kalshi and Polymarket prices and logs the result, so this is a record of what it believed and when, not a forecast.
