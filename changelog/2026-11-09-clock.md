The 2028 Machine, the clock of 2026-11-09

Days to South Carolina is now a model input. The DNC calendar puts the South Carolina primary on Jan. 22, 2028, which is 439 days after 2026-11-09. The nightly job and the page read the markets date, count the days left, and move two things in a straight line by days left, from today's values to their values on the eve of South Carolina, Jan. 21, 2028.

The random branch-event rate (the slider in the Advanced panel, 40 percent today) falls to a quarter of the slider's value. The model draws at most one such event per run, before the field forms, so the rate is the per-run chance of one. The prior draw spread falls from 30 percent at one sigma to 10 percent. Both hold at their end values after South Carolina. The clock starts on 2026-11-09, so today's numbers do not move. The page narrows a little every morning with no change to the code.
