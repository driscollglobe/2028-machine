The 2028 Machine, week of October 18, 2026

The biggest market moves between October 11, 2026 and October 18, 2026 were Jon Ossoff, down 1.8 points to 13.7, AOC, down 1.5 points to 14.4, Raphael Warnock, up 0.9 points to 2.5 and Kamala Harris, up 0.4 points to 7.5. Everyone else moved less than that, and most of the field did not move at all.

The model’s disagreement with the market changed most for Kamala Harris, where the gap between model share and market price went from 1.3 to 3.0, Gavin Newsom, where the gap between model share and market price went from 10.4 to 9.3 and Jon Ossoff, where the gap between model share and market price went from -4.9 to -4.0. A widening gap means the machine and the traders are reading the same news differently.

Threshold watch: Jon Ossoff fell below 10 in model share (10.6 to 9.7) and Kamala Harris crossed above 10 in model share (8.4 to 10.5). Five and ten are the lines where a candidate stops being a footnote and starts being a lane.

On the question of who actually runs, the Kalshi run market moved Jon Ossoff down 14.0 points to 52 percent, Kamala Harris up 9.0 points to 62 percent, Raphael Warnock up 6.0 points to 48 percent and Pete Buttigieg up 5.0 points to 91 percent.

The machine reruns 20,000 primaries every morning on that day’s Kalshi and Polymarket prices and logs the result, so this is a record of what it believed and when, not a forecast.
