The 2028 Machine, week of October 11, 2026

The biggest market moves between October 4, 2026 and October 11, 2026 were Gavin Newsom, down 1.3 points to 14.3, Raphael Warnock, up 1.0 points to 1.6, Jon Ossoff, down 0.8 points to 15.5 and James Talarico, down 0.6 points to 2.4. Everyone else moved less than that, and most of the field did not move at all.

The model’s disagreement with the market changed most for AOC, where the gap between model share and market price went from 22.1 to 20.3, Jon Ossoff, where the gap between model share and market price went from -6.5 to -4.9 and Gavin Newsom, where the gap between model share and market price went from 9.0 to 10.4. A widening gap means the machine and the traders are reading the same news differently.

Threshold watch: Jon Ossoff crossed above 10 in model share (9.8 to 10.6). Five and ten are the lines where a candidate stops being a footnote and starts being a lane.

On the question of who actually runs, the Kalshi run market moved Raphael Warnock up 9.0 points to 42 percent, Kamala Harris down 5.0 points to 53 percent, James Talarico up 5.0 points to 14 percent and Chris Murphy up 4.0 points to 67 percent.

The machine reruns 20,000 primaries every morning on that day’s Kalshi and Polymarket prices and logs the result, so this is a record of what it believed and when, not a forecast.
