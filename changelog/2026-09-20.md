The 2028 Machine, week of September 20, 2026

The biggest market moves between September 13, 2026 and September 20, 2026 were Gavin Newsom, up 1.9 points to 14.7, Kamala Harris, up 1.1 points to 8.9, Abdul El-Sayed, down 1.0 points to 1.0 and AOC, up 0.4 points to 17.1. Everyone else moved less than that, and most of the field did not move at all.

The model’s disagreement with the market changed most for Pete Buttigieg, where the gap between model share and market price went from 1.3 to -5.0, AOC, where the gap between model share and market price went from 19.6 to 24.6 and Gavin Newsom, where the gap between model share and market price went from 10.2 to 7.5. A widening gap means the machine and the traders are reading the same news differently.

Threshold watch: Pete Buttigieg fell below 5 in model share (6.7 to 0.4), Ro Khanna fell below 5 in model share (5.9 to 4.3) and Abdul El-Sayed crossed above 5 in model share (4.5 to 5.0). Five and ten are the lines where a candidate stops being a footnote and starts being a lane.

On the question of who actually runs, the Kalshi run market moved Kamala Harris down 18.0 points to 62 percent, Tim Walz up 15.0 points to 19 percent, Gavin Newsom down 8.0 points to 77 percent and Rahm Emanuel down 7.0 points to 78 percent.

The machine reruns 20,000 primaries every morning on that day’s Kalshi and Polymarket prices and logs the result, so this is a record of what it believed and when, not a forecast.
