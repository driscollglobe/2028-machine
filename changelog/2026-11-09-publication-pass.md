The 2028 Machine, publication pass of 2026-11-09

Lane crowding is now 0.05, down from 0.15. At 0.15 a third candidate in a lane cut everyone in it by about 23 percent, and the crowded Suburb and Fight lanes were cut so hard that Ro Khanna, alone in a thin lane when AOC stays out, finished first in 10.4 percent of default runs against a 2.2 market price. At 0.05 the cut for a third candidate is about 9 percent, and Khanna falls to 4.7 percent, fifth, with his operator base left at 5. Earlier on 2026-11-09 his base was lowered to 0.5 to get him under 6 percent; with crowding at 0.05 that is no longer needed, so the base is back at 5. The AI-issue lever is unchanged.

The calls ledger was reset before any call locked. The three calls are now: AOC's blended price above Ossoff's on any day through 2027-06-30; Ossoff's Kalshi run odds at 85 or more by 2027-06-30; Harris's poll minus market gap under 10 on any day through 2027-06-30. The poll gap is now part of the nightly data in default.json.

The field now narrows faster. Before this change 37.7 percent of default runs reached the convention without a pledged majority, well above anything in modern Democratic primaries, and the goal was under 15. Establishment coordination and dropout support transfer alone could not get there: across their full ranges the rate never fell below 27.7 (coordination 100, transfer 0), and more transfer raises it. So both slider defaults stay where they were (coordination 50, transfer 35) and two field rules changed instead. After Super Tuesday only the top two candidates by delegates go on to the late states, where it used to be three, which matches how the 2008, 2016 and 2020 races narrowed. A candidate now drops out after two contests under 11 percent of the vote, up from 7 (9 when coordination fails, up from 5). Steps on the 2026-11-09 books, 20,000 runs each, at crowding 0.15: top two alone 17.5; top two with the dropout line at 11 percent 13.2 (12.8 to 13.4 on four other seeds). With crowding then lowered to 0.05 the rate is 11.8. The methodology worked example keeps the old rules so it still reproduces.
