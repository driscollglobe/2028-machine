The 2028 Machine, week of November 1, 2026

The biggest market moves between October 25, 2026 and November 1, 2026 were Kamala Harris, down 0.7 points to 6.1, Gavin Newsom, up 0.6 points to 16.6, Jon Ossoff, down 0.6 points to 15.1 and AOC, down 0.5 points to 15.1. Everyone else moved less than that, and most of the field did not move at all.

The model’s disagreement with the market changed most for Jon Ossoff, where the gap between model share and market price went from -5.9 to -5.0, Gavin Newsom, where the gap between model share and market price went from 6.2 to 6.8 and Kamala Harris, where the gap between model share and market price went from 4.5 to 4.0. A widening gap means the machine and the traders are reading the same news differently.

Threshold watch: Jon Ossoff crossed above 10 in model share (9.8 to 10.1). Five and ten are the lines where a candidate stops being a footnote and starts being a lane.

On the question of who actually runs, the Kalshi run market moved Kamala Harris down 8.0 points to 58 percent, Gretchen Whitmer up 8.0 points to 38 percent, Rahm Emanuel up 5.0 points to 88 percent and Wes Moore down 4.0 points to 69 percent.

The machine reruns 20,000 primaries every morning on that day’s Kalshi and Polymarket prices and logs the result, so this is a record of what it believed and when, not a forecast.
