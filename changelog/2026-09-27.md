The 2028 Machine, week of September 27, 2026

The biggest market moves between September 20, 2026 and September 27, 2026 were Kamala Harris, down 2.2 points to 6.7, Andy Beshear, up 1.5 points to 4.3, Abdul El-Sayed, up 1.0 points to 2.0 and James Talarico, up 0.8 points to 3.0. Everyone else moved less than that, and most of the field did not move at all.

The model’s disagreement with the market changed most for Gavin Newsom, where the gap between model share and market price went from 7.5 to 9.2, AOC, where the gap between model share and market price went from 24.6 to 22.9 and Andy Beshear, where the gap between model share and market price went from -2.4 to -3.9. A widening gap means the machine and the traders are reading the same news differently.

Threshold watch: Jon Ossoff crossed above 10 in model share (9.6 to 10.1) and Abdul El-Sayed fell below 5 in model share (5.0 to 4.7). Five and ten are the lines where a candidate stops being a footnote and starts being a lane.

On the question of who actually runs, the Kalshi run market moved Wes Moore up 10.0 points to 79 percent, Kamala Harris down 9.0 points to 53 percent, Rahm Emanuel up 7.0 points to 85 percent and Gavin Newsom up 6.0 points to 83 percent.

The machine reruns 20,000 primaries every morning on that day’s Kalshi and Polymarket prices and logs the result, so this is a record of what it believed and when, not a forecast.
