Desk note, Nov. 18, 2026

Nobody moved more overnight than Gavin Newsom, who gained 0.5 points and now trades at 10.6. The two books split on it: Kalshi moved 1.0 point one way and Polymarket 0.1 points the other. Kalshi's market on whether El-Sayed runs at all dropped 12 points, to 13 percent, even though El-Sayed's nomination price barely moved. It didn't change much in the model, where Newsom finishes first in 21 percent of runs. AOC wins the most simulated primaries, 30 percent, though the betting favorite is Jon Ossoff at 26. About 10 in 100 simulated primaries end without a pledged majority, which sends the fight to the convention floor. Tonight's prices will show whether the Newsom move holds.
