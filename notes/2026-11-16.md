Desk note, Nov. 16, 2026

Gavin Newsom had the biggest move in the nomination markets overnight, up 0.5 points to 10.7. The move was all Kalshi, with Polymarket holding flat. The odds that Abdul El-Sayed enters the race fell 15 points on Kalshi, to 11 percent, even though El-Sayed's nomination price barely moved. Our simulations hardly noticed; Newsom still wins 21 percent of them. Jon Ossoff leads the markets at 26, but the model likes AOC, who wins 30 percent of simulated primaries. In 10 percent of those runs nobody reaches the convention with a pledged majority. Watch the Newsom price first when the books update tonight.
