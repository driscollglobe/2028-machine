Desk note, Nov. 27, 2026

Overnight, Jon Ossoff's price climbed 1.1 points to 23.5, the largest change on the board. All of it happened on Polymarket; Kalshi didn't budge. Kalshi's market on whether El-Sayed runs at all dropped 14 points, to 12 percent, even though El-Sayed's nomination price barely moved. It didn't change much in the model, where Ossoff finishes first in 17 percent of runs. Jon Ossoff leads the markets at 24, but the model likes AOC, who wins 31 percent of simulated primaries. In 10 percent of those runs nobody reaches the convention with a pledged majority. Tonight's prices will show whether the Ossoff move holds.
