Desk note, Nov. 24, 2026

AOC had the biggest move in the nomination markets overnight, up 3.0 points to 17.4. Both books agreed on the direction, with Polymarket moving 4.1 points and Kalshi 2.0 points. The odds that Abdul El-Sayed enters the race fell 19 points on Kalshi, to 8 percent, even though El-Sayed's nomination price barely moved. The model went along, and AOC now wins 32 percent of simulated primaries, against 31 the day before. AOC wins the most simulated primaries, 32 percent, though the betting favorite is Jon Ossoff at 22. In 11 percent of those runs nobody reaches the convention with a pledged majority. Tonight's prices will show whether the AOC move holds.
