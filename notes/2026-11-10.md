Desk note, Nov. 10, 2026

Nobody moved more overnight than Gavin Newsom, who gained 0.9 points and now trades at 11.1. All of it happened on Polymarket; Kalshi didn't budge. AOC wins the most simulated primaries, 27.2 percent, though the betting favorite is Jon Ossoff at 26.9. In 10 percent of those runs nobody reaches the convention with a pledged majority. Tonight's prices will show whether the Newsom move holds.
