Desk note, Nov. 10, 2026

Nobody moved more overnight than Gavin Newsom, who gained 0.5 points and now trades at 10.7. The two books split on it: Polymarket moved 2.0 points one way and Kalshi 1.0 point the other. The odds that Abdul El-Sayed enters the race fell 19 points on Kalshi, to 10 percent, even though El-Sayed's nomination price barely moved. AOC wins the most simulated primaries, 28 percent, though the betting favorite is Jon Ossoff at 26. In 11 percent of those runs nobody reaches the convention with a pledged majority. Tonight's prices will show whether the Newsom move holds.
