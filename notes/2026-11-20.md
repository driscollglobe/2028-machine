Desk note, Nov. 20, 2026

Jon Ossoff had the biggest move in the nomination markets overnight, down 0.7 points to 26.2. Most of that came on Kalshi, which moved 1.0 point, while Polymarket went the same way by 0.4 points. Kalshi's market on whether El-Sayed runs at all dropped 12 points, to 14 percent, even though El-Sayed's nomination price barely moved. In the simulations Ossoff went from 20 to 18 percent of first-place finishes. Jon Ossoff leads the markets at 26, but the model likes AOC, who wins 30 percent of simulated primaries. About 10 in 100 simulated primaries end without a pledged majority, which sends the fight to the convention floor. Watch the Ossoff price first when the books update tonight.
