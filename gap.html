The gap: polls versus markets

Polls measure name recognition this far out. Markets price the whole path to the nomination. The distance between them, candidate by candidate, is the story. Click a column to sort.

National Democratic primary polls, weighted, against the Kalshi and Polymarket blendloading
CandidatePoll averageMarket blendGapRun oddsNote
Poll average weights national Democratic primary polls from the last 90 days by recency (half as much every 30 days), by the square root of sample size, and by pollster quality and house adjustment from pollsters.csv. The methodology page has the details. Polls this far out track name recognition, and the model now adjusts for it: a candidate fewer voters know runs at a discount that shrinks as news coverage grows (how). Market blend is the simple average of the Kalshi and Polymarket nomination prices, updated nightly. Gap is poll minus market in points. Run odds are from the Kalshi run market. Notes are mine.
